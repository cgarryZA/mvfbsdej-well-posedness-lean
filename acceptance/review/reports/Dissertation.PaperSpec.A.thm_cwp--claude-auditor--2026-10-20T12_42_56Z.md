   unchanged.

   predicate; for Θ in 𝕊 it is automatic under (2.3) (s ↦ Law(Θ_s) is Borel into (P₂(H), W₂) for
   jointly measurable Θ), so no paper solution is excluded and the uniqueness covers all of 𝕊.

   augmented, every solution in 𝕊 has an indistinguishable representative of this kind, and 𝕊
   identifies indistinguishable processes.

8. Origin values in L²((0,T)) rather than L²(0,T) on the closed interval: the same space.
