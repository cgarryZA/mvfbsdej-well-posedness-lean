W, N and an independent initial σ-field F_0". The raw (un-augmented) generated filtration is
G_t = F_0 ∨ σ(W_s : s ≤ t) ∨ σ(N(B) : B ⊆ [0,t] × E measurable). In the Poisson namespace,
`naturalFiltration` presumably is the natural filtration of N (or of (W, N), possibly with
F_0): F^N_t = σ(N(B) : B ∈ B([0,t]) ⊗ E), equivalently σ(N((0,s] × A) : s ≤ t, A ∈ E,

Faithful Lean definition: the filtration whose time-t σ-algebra is generated by the
random variables N(B) (ℕ∪{∞}- or ℝ≥0∞-valued, measurable spaces with Borel structure) for
B measurable in [0,t] × E (or (0,t] × E — same σ-algebra up to the a.s. zero N({0}×E),
not identical without completion, but harmless after augmentation; similarly with or
without F_0 / W as specified by its docstring/usage). It need not be right-continuous or
complete (that is aug's job). It must be a filtration (monotone) and each σ-algebra must be
≤ the ambient one. If it includes W and F_0, those must enter as in the paper.
Whether the paper's filtration is aug (naturalFiltration ⊔ W-filtration ⊔ F_0) is a question
for the propositions' usage; the definition itself is faithful if it is the generated
filtration of what it names.
