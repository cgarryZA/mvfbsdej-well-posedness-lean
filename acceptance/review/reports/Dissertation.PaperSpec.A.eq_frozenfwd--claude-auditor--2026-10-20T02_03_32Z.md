Verdict: faithful with representation choices — Representation choices in eq_frozenfwd (each harmless):

1. Processes are coordinate families on all real times; the trial Z, U vanish off [0, T]; the
   trial X, Y have cadlag paths everywhere (paper: cadlag adapted; the filtration is augmented, so
   a null-set modification keeps adaptedness).
2. Law flows: exact laws lawP2 P (Theta_s) and lawP2 P (Theta^-_s) at every time (Dirac at 0 off
   L^2) instead of the Borel representatives of Lemma A.1; they agree for a.e. s, so the
   coefficient processes change only on a dt x dP-null set and no integral changes. The jump
   coefficient enters through a representative gamma of h(s, Theta^-_s, ., mu^-_s) in L^2(nu)
   for a.e. s, a.s. (the paper's reading via eq:fubiniiso).
3. The frozen equation is written with lambda psi + q^psi under one integral for each of the
   drift, Brownian and compensated Poisson integrals; equal to the paper's sum of two integrals by
   linearity, both being L^2 integrals of finite-energy integrands.
4. A solution of (4.7) (LinearFSDEJ) is adapted at every real time and right-continuous on every
   path at every real time, with the identity a.s. at each t in [0, T]. A paper solution on
   [0, T] is extended constantly off [0, T] and modified on a null set; for right-continuous
   sides the identity at each t is equivalent to one null set for all t. Existence is thus
   asserted with at least the paper's regularity, and uniqueness over a class containing every
   (normalised) paper solution, with possibly different representatives gamma.
5. The integrability conclusions are iterated lower integrals int^-_s int^-_omega in [0, inf];
   the integrands are jointly measurable (implied by the lambda = 1 instance of the existence
   clause, and by JointBorel), so by Tonelli they are E int_0^T |psi|^2 ds.
6. The Brownian and compensated Poisson integrals are the L^2 integrals; under Assumption 2.2
   all coefficient processes along Theta have finite energy, so this is the paper's setting.
7. Hypothesis encodings: Assumption22 (origin values in L^2 on (0, T), Dirac law P2.dirac 0),
   Assumption23 with the Hilbert-norm W_2 and Frobenius / L^2(nu) norms, PredForcing for the
   paper's forcing class (q^h marked predictable, all components vanishing off [0, T]).
