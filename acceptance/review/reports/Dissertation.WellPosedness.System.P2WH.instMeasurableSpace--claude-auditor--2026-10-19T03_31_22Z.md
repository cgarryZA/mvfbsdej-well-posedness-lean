Reading of def P2WH.instMeasurableSpace (used by eq:coefmeas and Lemma A.1).
Paper: (P_2(H), W_2) is a Polish metric space (Villani Ch. 6); law flows are Borel maps
mu: [0,T] -> (P_2(H), W_2); coefficients are jointly Borel in (t,theta,mu). So the measurable space on
P_2(H) must be the Borel sigma-algebra of the W_2 metric topology. Equivalent alternatives: the trace on
P_2(H) of the weak-topology Borel sigma-algebra, or the sigma-algebra generated by mu |-> mu(A), A Borel in H
(these coincide on P_2 of a Polish space by Lusin-Souslin, since the W_2 topology is Polish and finer than
the weak one). A faithful Lean instance is one of these (borel of the W_2 metric being the direct reading).
Something coarser (e.g. trivial sigma-algebra) or finer (e.g. discrete/top) would change the meaning of
"jointly Borel" and "Borel law flow".
