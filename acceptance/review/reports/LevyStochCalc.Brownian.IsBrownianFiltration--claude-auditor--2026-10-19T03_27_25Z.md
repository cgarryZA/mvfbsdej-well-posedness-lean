Paper notion (sec 2): W is a Brownian motion WITH RESPECT TO the filtration (F_t): the filtration
is the usual augmentation of the one generated by W, N and F_0 (W, N, F_0 independent), so W is
an (F_t)-Brownian motion: 
 (a) W is (F_t)-adapted (W_t is F_t-measurable);
 (b) for s <= t, the increment W_t - W_s is independent of F_s
 (with (a),(b) and the BM law, W is an (F_t)-martingale with quadratic variation t I_d - what the
 Ito integral / PRP / BDG need).
A faithful Lean predicate IsBrownianFiltration F W must contain (a) and (b) (or an equivalent:
"W_{t+s}-W_s independent of F_s"). It need not encode "F is generated by W, N, F_0" (that is
part of the setting rather than of the predicate name) - if the predicate claims to be the
paper's filtration hypothesis but omits generation, note it, since the PRP fails for a larger
filtration. A weaker predicate (adaptedness only) is NOT the paper's notion (the Ito isometry and
martingale property would fail).
