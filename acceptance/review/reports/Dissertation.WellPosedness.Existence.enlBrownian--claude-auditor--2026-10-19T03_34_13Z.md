Reading of def Existence.enlBrownian (Example 5.2: "the filtration is generated by W and F_0").

Paper notion. The Brownian analogue of the sec. 2 filtration: (F_t) is the usual augmentation of
the filtration generated by W and the independent initial sigma-field F_0, i.e.
F_t = intersection_{u>t} sigma(F_0 v sigma(W_s : s <= u) v N_P), N_P the P-null sets (for a
Brownian filtration with independent initial enlargement, the completed filtration
sigma(F_0, W_s: s <= t, N_P) is already right-continuous, so completion alone gives the same
filtration). The name suggests "enlarged Brownian filtration": the initial enlargement of the
Brownian filtration by F_0.
A faithful Lean definition: a filtration indexed by time (R_{>=0} or [0,T]) whose t-th
sigma-algebra is generated by F_0 and W_s for s <= t, together with the P-null sets (and
right-continuous regularisation, or equality with it). Edge cases: if the Lean omits the null
sets (raw natural filtration), it differs from the usual augmentation; that is acceptable only if
every use is insensitive (e.g. adaptedness up to modification) - otherwise a representation
choice to be argued. It must not include any Poisson information. F_0 is a given sigma-field
(sub-sigma-algebra) independent of W; the definition itself need not assert independence.
