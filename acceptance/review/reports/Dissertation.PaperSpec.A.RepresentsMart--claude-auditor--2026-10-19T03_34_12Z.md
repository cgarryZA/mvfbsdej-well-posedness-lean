Reading of def PaperSpec.A.RepresentsMart (sec. 2 PRP paragraph, 02-model-assumptions.tex:47-56).

Paper notion. A square-integrable (F_t)-martingale M (R^m- or R-valued) on [0,T] is represented
by (Z,U) when
  M_t = M_0 + int_0^t Z_s dW_s + int_0^t int_E U_s(e) Ntilde(ds,de)   for every t in [0,T] (a.s.),
with (Z,U) a predictable square-integrable pair: Z predictable R^{m x d} (or 1 x d) valued with
E int_0^T |Z|_F^2 dt < inf; U predictable (P (x) E-measurable, or predictable L^2(nu)-valued via
(2.3)) with E int_0^T ||U_t||^2_{L^2(nu)} dt < inf; M_0 F_0-measurable. The stochastic integrals
are the L^2 Ito integral w.r.t. W and the compensated Poisson integral w.r.t. Ntilde = N - dt nu,
for the filtration of sec. 2.
A faithful Lean definition must contain: the predictability and square integrability of Z, U
(or these must be supplied at every use, e.g. inside an existential that includes them);
the identity at every t in [0,T] a.s. (or indistinguishability); the right integrals; no extra
constraint on M beyond what the paper has. The F_0-measurability of M_0 can be in the PRP
statement instead (it is automatic for adapted M). Junk: if the integrals take junk value 0 on
non-integrable integrands, the integrability fields are essential (otherwise any M with
M_t = M_0 + 0 would be "represented" by a non-integrable pair, making uniqueness false and
existence trivial in degenerate cases).
