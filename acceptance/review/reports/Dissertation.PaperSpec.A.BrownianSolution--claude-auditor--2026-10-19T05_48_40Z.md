Example 5.2: "Remove the Poisson channel from the stochastic basis, so that the filtration is
generated by W and F_0 and the representation property is the Brownian one. Delete the
U-coordinate from the tuple, reducing H to R^n x R^m x R^{m x d}. Merely setting h = 0 on a
Brownian-Poisson basis is not a reduction, since the backward Poisson integral and its integrand
U persist in the representation."

So a Brownian solution is: on a filtered probability space with usual conditions carrying a
d-dim Brownian motion W, filtration = usual augmentation of the one generated by W and an
independent F_0, horizon T>0; coefficients b: [0,T] x H_B x P2(H_B) -> R^n,
sigma: -> R^{n x d}, f: -> R^m, g: R^n x P2(R^n) -> R^m, deterministic, Borel, with
H_B = R^n x R^m x R^{m x d} (Euclidean/Frobenius norm); a triple Theta = (X,Y,Z) with
X in S2(R^n), Y in S2(R^m) (cadlag adapted, E sup^2 finite), Z in H2_W (predictable, or
progressive, square-integrable), such that for every t in [0,T] a.s.
  X_t = xi_0 + int_0^t b(s,Theta_s,mu_s) ds + int_0^t sigma(s,Theta_s,mu_s) dW_s,
  Y_t = g(X_T, Law X_T) + int_t^T f(s,Theta_s,mu_s) ds - int_t^T Z_s dW_s,
with mu_s = Law(X_s,Y_s,Z_s) in P2(H_B). No jump coefficient, no U, no Poisson integral.

What a faithful Lean definition must be: one of
 (A) a separate notion over a Brownian basis (no Poisson random measure, filtration generated
     by W and F_0), triple (X,Y,Z), the two identities above; or
 (B) the general solution notion of (1.1) on a basis with nu = 0 (the paper itself identifies
     nu = 0 with the Brownian case in Remark 2.9: L2(nu) = {0}, U = 0, Ntilde = 0, and the
     filtration generated by W, N, F_0 is the one generated by W, F_0), with the trivial U
     coordinate and P2(H_B x {0}) = P2(H_B) as harmless representation choices.
NOT faithful: a Brownian-Poisson basis with nu != 0 and h = 0 (the paper says explicitly this is
not the reduction), or keeping a nontrivial U-coordinate / Poisson integral in the backward
equation, or a filtration bigger than the Brownian one (then the Brownian representation
property fails and the notion is a different one), unless the propositions only use it on the
paper's basis. Same edge cases as for Solution: junk integrals, Euclidean norms, joint law under
P of the triple, a.s.-for-each-t identities, adaptedness to the Brownian filtration.
