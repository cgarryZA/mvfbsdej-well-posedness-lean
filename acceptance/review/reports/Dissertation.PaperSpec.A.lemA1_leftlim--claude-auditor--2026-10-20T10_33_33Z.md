Setting (standing section 2 + hypotheses of Lemma A.1): a filtered probability space (Omega, F, (F_t), P) with the usual
conditions, horizon T > 0, dimensions n, m >= 1. The Brownian motion / Poisson measure / nu play no role in this clause.
Hypotheses of Lemma A.1 relevant here: X : time x Omega -> R^n and Y : time x Omega -> R^m are adapted with cadlag paths
(every path, or almost every path), and E int_0^T (|X_t|^2 + |Y_t|^2) dt < infinity. (The Z, U part of the lemma's
integrability hypothesis is in force but not used; carrying it or dropping it is harmless, dropping makes the statement
broader with the printed one as the instance.)

Conclusions a faithful statement must contain, for X and likewise for Y:
 (a) the left-limit process X_-(t, omega) := lim_{s up t} X_s(omega) (with the usual convention X_{0-} := X_0, or any
     F_0-measurable / deterministic value at t = 0) is measurable for the predictable sigma-algebra on [0,T] x Omega
     (generated by the adapted left-continuous processes, equivalently by {0} x F_0 and (s,t] x F_s);
 (b) on every (cadlag) path the jump set {t in [0,T] : X_{t-} != X_t} is countable and has Lebesgue measure zero,
     i.e. int_0^T 1{X_{t-} != X_t} dt = 0 (the paper says "almost surely"; "on every path" is the same when every
     path is cadlag, and is a harmless strengthening);
 (c) X_- = X for (Leb on [0,T]) (x) P - almost every (t, omega);
 (d) E int_0^T |X_{t-}|^2 dt < infinity ("the hypothesis on X, Y thus passes to them").
No constants. The left limit must be the genuine left limit of the path (junk values of a total `limUnder` are only
acceptable at t = 0 / on non-cadlag paths, where they must not break (a)). The predictable sigma-algebra must be the one
of the filtration (F_t) of the standing basis (or of an arbitrary filtration, which is broader). Witness: X, Y with
genuine jumps (e.g. a compensated Poisson process), so that X_- != X somewhere.
