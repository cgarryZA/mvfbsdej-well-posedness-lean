Reading (locked before any Lean is shown) — def LevyStochCalc.Probability.aug

Paper notion (§2): "a filtered probability space satisfying the usual conditions ... The
filtration (F_t) is the usual augmentation of the one generated by W, N and an independent
initial σ-field F_0." The usual augmentation of a filtration (G_t) under P: F_t =
∩_{s > t} σ(G_s ∪ 𝒩), with 𝒩 the P-null sets (of the completed F, or of F_∞); this is
right-continuous and contains all null sets (equivalently σ(G_{t+} ∪ 𝒩)).

Faithful Lean definition: for each t, the σ-algebra ⨅_{s > t} (G_s ⊔ σ(null sets)), or
(⨅_{s>t} G_s) ⊔ σ(null sets), with null sets taken as the P-null measurable sets of the
ambient σ-algebra (since Lean's Ω carries one σ-algebra, adding null sets of the completion
not measurable in the ambient space is not possible; adding ambient-measurable null sets is
the standard Lean-level surrogate and is fine provided the ambient σ-algebra is what all
processes are measured against). Edge cases: at the end of the time index (if the index is
bounded, e.g. [0,T], ⨅ over the empty set gives ⊤ — must be capped at the ambient σ-algebra,
i.e. ≤ the ambient σ-algebra, otherwise it is not a filtration of the measurable space);
right-continuity must hold; null sets of P (not of some other measure). If aug only
completes (no right limit), the "usual conditions" claim fails (narrower: then results using
right-continuity, e.g. debut/stopping-time arguments, would be unavailable, but for the
statements it changes which processes are adapted).
