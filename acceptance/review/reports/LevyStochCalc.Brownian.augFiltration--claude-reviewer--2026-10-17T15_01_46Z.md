Paper notion. Section 2, lines 222-229: the filtered space satisfies the usual conditions, and (F_t) is
"the usual augmentation" of the filtration generated by W, N and F_0. The usual augmentation of a
filtration (G_t) under P: F_t = intersection over u > t of sigma(G_u, null sets), where the null sets
are the P-null sets (of the completion of the ambient sigma-algebra, or of F); equivalently the
smallest filtration containing G that is complete (F_0 contains all P-null sets) and right-continuous.
For a filtration generated by a Levy process (here (W,N)) together with an independent F_0, adding the
null sets already yields right-continuity (Blumenthal-type 0-1 law), so "augmentation by null sets"
and "usual augmentation" coincide in the paper's setting.

What a faithful Lean definition must be.
- Given a filtration G and the probability P: F_t = G_t joined with the sigma-algebra generated by the
  P-null sets (sets of P-measure zero), possibly followed by right-continuous regularisation
  (intersection over u > t). Either is acceptable for the paper's generated filtration, by the remark
  above, provided the propositions are about that filtration; on a general filtration only the version
  with right-continuity is the usual augmentation.
- Lean caveat: the ambient MeasurableSpace is fixed and need not be complete. If "null sets" means sets
  of outer measure zero not necessarily measurable in the ambient sigma-algebra, F_t may fail to be
  <= the ambient sigma-algebra, so it would not be a Mathlib Filtration on that space; a faithful Lean
  version must either use null sets that are measurable (P-null sets in the ambient sigma-algebra) or
  work on a complete space. Using only measurable null sets is the augmentation relative to F, which is
  what the standard definition gives when F is complete, and is harmless otherwise because the
  measures of all relevant events and the a.s. statements are unchanged.
- The augmentation must be by P-null sets for THE probability measure of the paper (not e.g. an
  arbitrary measure), and must contain G_t.
