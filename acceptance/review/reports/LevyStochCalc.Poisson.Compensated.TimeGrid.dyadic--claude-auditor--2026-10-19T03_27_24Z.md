Reading.
The paper has no notion of a time grid: the compensated integral is the standard L2 integral,
constructed (in any standard reference) as the isometric extension of the elementary integral of
step integrands on finite partitions 0 = t_0 < t_1 < ... < t_N of the time axis. A "TimeGrid"
structure is an auxiliary of that construction. It is faithful if it encodes a finite partition
of the time axis (points starting at 0, nondecreasing or strictly increasing, finitely many) used
for left-open right-closed intervals (t_i, t_{i+1}]; any grid of this kind is admissible (the
construction must not depend on a particular grid for its final value). Its fields carry no
meaning of the paper beyond that; what matters is that the integral built on top
(stochasticIntegral) is the paper integral.

Specific to dyadic: the dyadic grid of level n (points i 2^{-n} or i 2^{-n} T, for i up to the
horizon), the standard approximating sequence of the construction: nested, with mesh tending to
0 as n -> infinity, covering the horizon. Faithful if it is such a grid; the precise
normalization is a representation choice.
