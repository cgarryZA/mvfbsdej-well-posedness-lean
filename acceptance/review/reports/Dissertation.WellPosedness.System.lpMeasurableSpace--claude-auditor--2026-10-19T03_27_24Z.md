Paper notion. The sigma-algebra on L^2(nu;R^m) (and on L^2(nu;R^n) for h, and hence on H) used to
speak of measurability of U-valued random variables, of laws of tuples, of P_2(H) and of Borel
measurability of coefficients (sec 2: "All coefficients are jointly Borel measurable";
(2.hborel) "Borel measurability of the map (t,theta,mu) |-> h(t,theta,.,mu) ... -> L^2(nu;R^n)").
The paper's sigma-algebra is the BOREL sigma-algebra of the norm topology of L^2(nu) (separable
since E is countably generated and nu sigma-finite, so Borel = generated by the evaluations
against a dense countable set, and strongly measurable = Borel measurable).
A faithful Lean instance: MeasurableSpace (Lp _ 2 nu) := borel (Lp _ 2 nu) (with BorelSpace
holding), not the trivial sigma-algebras (top: every set measurable - would make measurability
conditions vacuous and laws of non-Borel maps definable; bot: only trivial sets), and not some
other generated sigma-algebra unless it equals Borel.
