intensity is nu(ds,dq) = c_s q^{-1-rho} dq, the dq being Lebesgue measure on the size interval

endpoints are Lebesgue-null). It must be the plain dq (no density, no normalisation other than the
Lebesgue one), finite of total mass 1, sigma-finite; the q^{-1-rho} density and side constants are
applied elsewhere (in the mark measure). If instead sizeLeb already carries the density, it must be
exactly q^{-1-rho} dq on (0,1] (infinite mass).
