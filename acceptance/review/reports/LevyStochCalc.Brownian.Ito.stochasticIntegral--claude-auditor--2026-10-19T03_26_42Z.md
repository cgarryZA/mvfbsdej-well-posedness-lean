Paper notion: the (scalar) Ito integral process t |-> int_0^t Z_s dW_s of a square-integrable
integrand Z against a (1-dimensional) Brownian motion W, relative to the filtration of the
stochastic basis (sec. 2/3). It is the L^2 extension of the simple integral (isometry
E|int_0^t Z dW|^2 = E int_0^t |Z|^2 ds), a square-integrable martingale with a continuous version;
in the paper's solution identities it appears at each fixed t, a.s. (sec. 3: "both identities
a.s. for every t"), and in sup-estimates via BDG/Doob (which needs a cadlag/continuous version
or at least a modification argument).
Faithful Lean: a function of (t, omega) whose value at each t is (a representative of) the L^2
limit of the simple integrals up to t along an energy-approximating sequence, for every Z in the
paper's class (predictable / progressively measurable, adapted to the filtration w.r.t. which W
is a Brownian motion, E int_0^T |Z|^2 < oo). Requirements:
 - the filtration with respect to which adaptedness of Z is required must be the one the paper
   uses (the augmented filtration generated by W, N, F_0), or a parameter the propositions set
   to it; if the integral is tied to W's own natural filtration, integrands adapted to the larger
   filtration (depending on N or F_0) would fall outside the class and be junk -- not faithful
   for the paper's use;
 - the W-process must be a Brownian motion with respect to that filtration (independent
   increments of F_s), else the isometry fails;
 - junk value (e.g. 0) outside the class is harmless only if every proposition restricts to the
   class;
 - choosing a representative per t (no joint version) is acceptable for "a.s. for every t"
   identities but sup-in-t statements then need an explicit cadlag version.

Paper context (sec. 2, sec. 3, sec. 4, App. A). Standing basis: (Omega,F,(F_t),P) satisfying the usual
conditions, horizon T>0, d-dimensional Brownian motion W and an independent Poisson random
measure N on R_+ x E with intensity dt nu(de), nu sigma-finite on a countably generated (E,E);
(F_t) is the usual augmentation of the filtration generated by W, N and an independent initial
sigma-field F_0. Stochastic integrals int Z dW (Z in H^2_W: R^{m x d}-valued predictable,
E int_0^T |Z|_F^2 dt < oo) and int int U dNtilde (U in H^2_N) are the L^2 (Ito /
compensated-Poisson isometry) integrals; they are true L^2 martingales, cadlag, and are used in
identities holding a.s. for every t (sec. 3, "solution"). The paper never constructs these
integrals itself: it takes the standard construction (Applebaum sec. 4, He-Wang-Yan). So every
unit below is infrastructure: the paper's notion is the textbook one, and a faithful Lean
definition is one that (together with the other definitions) yields the textbook object on the
paper's basis for every integrand the paper's claims use; junk values outside that class are
harmless only if every proposition restricts to the class.
