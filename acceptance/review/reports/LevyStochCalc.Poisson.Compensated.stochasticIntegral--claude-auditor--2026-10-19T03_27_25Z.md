of the filtration generated by W, N and an independent initial sigma-field F_0. The compensated
measure is Ntilde(dt,de) = N(dt,de) - dt nu(de). Integrands U of the Poisson integral are
predictable: P (x) calE-measurable, P the predictable sigma-field on Omega x [0,T]
(eq:fubiniiso), with E int_0^T ||U_t||^2_{L2(nu)} dt < infinity (the space H^2_N). The
integral int_0^t int_E U_s(e) Ntilde(ds,de) is the standard L2 (Ito-Levy) compensated-Poisson
integral: an L2 martingale, cadlag, with the isometry E|int_0^t int U dNtilde|^2 =
E int_0^t ||U_s||^2 ds (used in Lemma 4.1 "true L2-martingales by the Ito and
compensated-Poisson isometries"), jumps Delta = U_s(e) at atoms (s,e) of N, and for integrands
in L1(dP dt nu) the integral splits as int U dN - int U nu ds (eq:compid, eq:zeromean).

q^b, q^sigma, q^f (eq:coefmeas, section 4); predictability (P (x) calE) for h and q^h.

This is the paper's integral int_0^t int_E U_s(e) Ntilde(ds,de) itself (the jump terms of the
forward and backward equations of (eq:mvfbsdej), (eq:forced), Lemma 4.1, the PRP display of
section 2, and the dealer example's linear equation). A faithful Lean definition: for an integrand
U : (time) x Omega x E -> R (componentwise for R^m / R^n valued U) that is predictable
(P (x) calE-measurable, with P the predictable sigma-field of the setting's filtration) and has
E int_0^T int_E |U|^2 dnu ds < infinity, a process (t, omega) -> I_t(U)(omega) such that
 (a) for each t, I_t(U) = the L2(Omega)-limit of elementary integrals
     sum xi Ntilde((a ^ t, b ^ t] x B) of step integrands converging to U in H^2_N
     (Ntilde = N - dt x nu on finite-intensity cells), so that the isometry
     E|I_t(U)|^2 = E int_0^t ||U_s||^2 ds and linearity hold;
 (b) t -> I_t(U)(omega) is cadlag (a fixed version; the paper's solution identities are a.s.
     for every t and both sides cadlag), adapted, an L2 martingale;
 (c) for step integrands it reduces to the pathwise formula.
The integral is determined only up to indistinguishability; any version with (a)-(b) is
faithful. Junk values (e.g. 0) for U outside H^2_N or not predictable are harmless only if every
proposition using the integral imposes U in H^2_N (predictable, square integrable) on its
integrand; a junk value would silently make a "solution" identity hold with a wrong integral
otherwise. Dependence on N only through the PRM and the filtration with respect to which U is
predictable: the integral must be the one relative to the filtration of the setting (an
(F_t)-Poisson random measure). Time domain [0,infinity) or [0,T] both acceptable.
