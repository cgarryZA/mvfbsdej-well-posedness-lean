Verdict: faithful with representation choices — Representation choices shared by eq_fwd, eq_wgt and eq_bwd (each harmless):

1. Processes are coordinate families of real processes indexed by all real times
   (X i t omega, Z k t omega j, U k t omega e); Z, U and the forcings are required to vanish off
   [0, T] and a predictable Z (LevyStochCalc Probability.Predictable, whose sigma-algebra on
   (-inf, 0] only has the cells (-inf, 0] x A) is therefore 0 at time 0. Only [0, T] enters the
   equations and the norms, and Z, U are identified dt x dP(-x nu)-a.e. in the paper, so every
   paper solution has such a representative.
2. Cadlag: every path of X, Y is right-continuous on [0, T) and has left limits on (0, T]
   (WeakForcedSolution), the identities of (4.1) holding on one full-measure set for all
   t in [0, T] with cadlag versions of the integrals. The filtration enlJoint is the usual
   augmentation (null sets in the time-0 field), so an a.s.-cadlag paper solution is modified on
   a null set without losing adaptedness; the paper itself notes the exceptional set may be
   taken independent of t.
3. Law flows: mu_t is the exact law lawP2 P (Theta_t) at every time (Dirac at 0 where Theta_t is
   not square integrable), and mu^-_t the exact law of the predictable tuple (leftLimProc X,
   leftLimProc Y, Z, U). They agree with the paper's Borel representatives (Lemma A.1) for a.e. t,
   so the coefficient processes change only on dt x dP-null sets and no integral changes. The
   jump coefficient enters through a representative gamma of h(s, Theta^-_s, ., mu^-_s) in
   L^2(nu) for a.e. s, a.s. (the paper's reading via eq:fubiniiso).
4. Stochastic integrals are the L^2 integrals (finite energy of the forced diffusion and jump
   integrands lambda psi + q^psi); the drift and driver integrands need only a.s. path
   integrability, which is right since Lemma 4.3 does not assume Assumption 2.2. For sigma this
   is equivalent, under Assumption 2.3, to the existence of the Ito integral at all; for the
   jump channel it is the paper's own reading (section 2: Poisson integrals of square-integrable
   predictable integrands read through a P x E representative).
5. Expectations are lower Lebesgue integrals in [0, inf] (esq = sum of squared coordinates,
   energy / markedEnergy = E int_0^T |.|^2 (nu)); the constant is ENNReal.ofReal (K(...)) with
   K : R^7 -> R. A Lean K gives the paper's positive C := max(K, 1), and a C of the paper gives K := C,
   so the existential claims are equivalent.
