Verdict: faithful with representation choices — Representation choices, each harmless:
1. Linear (frozen) form with given integrands b, σ, γ: the affine-in-X equations of the paper are
   instances (integrands built from X), as the coordinatewise forward difference equation is.
2. Scalar unknown; ℝⁿ-valued equations are imposed coordinatewise with the common driver.
3. The jump integrand is marked progressively measurable instead of predictable (e.g. X_s for
   X_{s−}): the compensated integral is the L² isometric one, which depends only on the
   dP⊗ds⊗ν-class, and such a γ has a predictable version a.e., which is how the paper reads every
   Poisson integral (§2).
4. Integrands extended by 0 off [0,T], with finite energy on [0,T] (= the paper's L²).
5. Solution class "adapted, all paths right-continuous, identity a.s. at each t ∈ [0,T]" in place
   of "càdlàg adapted in S²": with right-continuity, the identity on a countable dense set of
   times and Doob's inequality give a.s. left limits and E sup|X|² < ∞, and every paper solution
   with càdlàg paths satisfies it.
6. ξ₀ only required square integrable; its F_0-measurability a.e. follows from X_0 = ξ₀ a.s.
7. The filtration is any ℱ for which W is Brownian and N Poisson; the paper's augmented
   filtration (and the enlarged one) are instances.
