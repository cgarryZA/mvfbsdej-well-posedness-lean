Reading for def:LevyStochCalc.Brownian.IsBrownianFiltration (written before seeing any Lean).

Paper notion. Section 2: (F_t) is the usual augmentation of the filtration generated by W, N and
an independent initial sigma-field F_0, with W a d-dimensional BM independent of N. What the
Brownian stochastic integral needs, and what this setting provides, is that W is an
(F_t)-Brownian motion: (i) W_t is F_t-measurable for every t (adapted); (ii) for s <= t the
increment W_t - W_s is independent of F_s. (The filtration is not the natural filtration of W:
it also contains N and F_0; so a predicate requiring F to be generated by W would be too strong
and would exclude the paper's setting, unless the Brownian--Poisson case is formalised with a
different predicate.)

A faithful Lean predicate "IsBrownianFiltration" (relating a filtration F and a BM W) must:
- assert adaptedness of W to F and independence of future increments W_t - W_s (s <= t) from
  F_s, with respect to the reference measure P;
- be satisfiable by the paper's filtration (augmented, containing N and F_0); it should not
  require F to be the natural filtration of W or right-continuity/completeness beyond what the
  usual conditions give (usual conditions are fine);
- independence must be of the increment from the whole sigma-algebra F_s (not just from W_s).
