Paper notion. Section 2 (lines 224-229): W is a d-dimensional BM "and an independent Poisson random
measure N", with "an independent initial sigma-field F_0"; the filtration is generated by W, N and F_0.
The sigma-field generated by the Brownian motion is sigma(W) = sigma(W_s : s >= 0) (all times, all
components), used to express the independence of W from N and F_0; a time-indexed variant
sigma(W_s : s <= t) is the Brownian part of the generated filtration.

What a faithful Lean definition must be.
- The sigma-algebra on Omega generated by the BM: supremum over s (in the time index, possibly s <= t if
  time-indexed) and over components j of comap W^j_s (Borel). For independence purposes it must be the
  whole-path sigma-algebra (all s >= 0, or at least all s <= T), not one time.
- Raw (no null sets); <= ambient when each W_s is measurable.
- If it is time-indexed, it should be monotone in t and equal the natural filtration of W (unit 07).
