Reading.
The paper uses only the finished integral int_0^t int_E U_s(e) Ntilde(ds,de) for U in H^2_N
(predictable, P (x) calE-measurable, E int_0^T ||U_s||^2 ds < infinity), with the standard
properties: for each t it is the L2(Omega)-limit of elementary integrals of step integrands
approximating U in H^2_N (the isometric extension, independent of the approximating sequence);
the process t -> int_0^t is taken in a cadlag version (the paper uses its jumps, running suprema
via Doob/BDG, and "both sides of each identity are cadlag in t"), an (F_t) square-integrable
martingale, determined up to indistinguishability. Any construction of it (staged
approximations, horizon-by-horizon pieces, a.s.-convergent fast subsequences, a "master"
cadlag limit) is an implementation choice of layer 2. An auxiliary of the construction is
faithful when it is a correct step towards a final integral with these properties, and no junk
value of it (a default for non-integrable U, a limit taken where the sequence does not converge,
a toReal of infinity) reaches the integral on the class of integrands the propositions use;
outside H^2_N (or on non-predictable U) any junk value is harmless provided every proposition
using the integral imposes membership in H^2_N on its integrands (as the paper does: U in H^2_N,
h along a solution in L2).

Specific to stageIntegral: the elementary (step) compensated integral at stage n, i.e. the
integral of the n-th step approximation of U, as a process in t: sum over the cells of
coefficient times Ntilde((a ^ t, b ^ t] x B) with finite-mass mark cells B. Faithful if it is
that elementary integral (pathwise formula N(cell) - (dt x nu)(cell)) of a step integrand that
approximates U in H^2_N.
