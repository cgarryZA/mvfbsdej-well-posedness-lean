Reading.
Paper notion (section 2): "an independent Poisson random measure N on R_+ x E with intensity
dt nu(de), where nu is a sigma-finite measure on a countably generated (E, calE)"; "the
infinite-activity regime, in which N has infinitely many atoms on every nondegenerate time
interval, is admitted throughout" (so nu(E) = infinity must be allowed, and then sets of the
form (a,b] x E have infinite intensity). The standard definition (Ikeda-Watanabe, Applebaum
2.3, Kallenberg): a map omega -> N(omega) into measures on R_+ x E (counting measures), with
 (a) for every measurable A, omega -> N(omega)(A) is a [0, infinity]-valued random variable;
 (b) for every measurable A, N(A) is Poisson with mean lambda(A) := (dt x nu)(A), with the
     convention Poisson(infinity) = delta_infinity (so N(A) = infinity a.s. when lambda(A) =
     infinity); at least for every A with lambda(A) < infinity;
 (c) for pairwise disjoint measurable A_1, ..., A_k, the variables N(A_1), ..., N(A_k) are
     independent (mutually, not merely pairwise).
Integer-valuedness of N(A) for finite-intensity A follows a.s. from (b). Faithful Lean: a
structure carrying a random measure (Omega -> Measure (time x E), or a kernel) with (a)-(c),
intensity the product of Lebesgue measure on the time half-line and nu. Representation choices
that are harmless: time domain R_{>=0} (NNReal) or [0, infinity) in R; ENNReal-valued counts;
measurability of omega -> N(omega) as a measure-valued map instead of (a); (b) required only for
finite-intensity sets IF the infinite-intensity sets are not needed (they are automatically
infinite a.s. when they are increasing unions of finite-intensity disjoint pieces, by (b)-(c)
and Borel-Cantelli / sum of independent Poissons). Unfaithful: if the Poisson law used for
infinite intensity is a junk value (e.g. delta_0 via toNNReal infinity = 0), the structure
cannot exist when nu(E) = infinity, so every proposition that quantifies over a PRM with
infinite-activity nu would be vacuous; if independence is only pairwise; if the intensity is not
Leb x nu. Independence of N from W and F_0 is not part of the notion "PRM" (it is a separate
hypothesis of the setting).

Specific to the field N: the random measure itself, omega -> N(omega), a measure on
(time) x E for each omega (the counting measure of the atoms). Its meaning is that of the
structure; the field is faithful as the carrier of the random measure.
