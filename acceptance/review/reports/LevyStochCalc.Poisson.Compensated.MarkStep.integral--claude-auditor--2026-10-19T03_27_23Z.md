Reading.
The paper never names a "mark step" object: it uses the compensated integral only through the
standard L2 theory, whose elementary integrands are
  U_s(omega,e) = sum_{i,k} xi_{ik}(omega) 1_{(t_i,t_{i+1}]}(s) 1_{B_k}(e),
with a finite time grid t_0 < ... , each xi_{ik} F_{t_i}-measurable (bounded, or at least
square-integrable), and finitely many mark sets B_k in calE with nu(B_k) < infinity; their
integral is sum_{i,k} xi_{ik} Ntilde((t_i ^ t, t_{i+1} ^ t] x B_k), where
Ntilde(A) = N(A) - (dt x nu)(A) is meaningful only for finite-intensity A. A structure
"MarkStep" (a simple integrand in the mark variable, possibly also in time) is faithful when its
fields jointly encode such an integrand: the time intervals left-open right-closed (predictable),
the coefficients measurable at the left endpoint of their interval (with respect to the
filtration of the setting), the mark cells measurable with finite nu-mass (else Ntilde is not
defined and junk values enter), finitely many cells; and when the evaluation and integral built
from it give the formulas above. This unit is an auxiliary of the construction of the paper
integral; it is faithful if it is a correct component of that construction (the final
stochasticIntegral agreeing with the paper integral), and unfaithful if a junk value or a
missing measurability/finite-mass condition can reach the integral the propositions use.

Specific to integral: the elementary compensated integral of the step integrand,
  integral(omega) (up to time t) = sum_k xi_k(omega) Ntilde((a_k ^ t, b_k ^ t] x B_k)(omega),
Ntilde(A) = N(A) - (dt x nu)(A), with nu(B_k) < infinity so that each term is a real-valued,
a.s. finite random variable (N(A) < infinity a.s. for finite-intensity A). It must coincide with
int_0^t int_E eval dNtilde of the paper. A real-valued encoding (toReal of the ENNReal counts)
is harmless when the cells have finite intensity (the junk value on the null set where N(A) =
infinity does not matter a.s.); it is unfaithful if the mark cells may have infinite nu-mass.
