 filtration generated by W, N (restricted to [0,t] x E) and F_0. Poisson random measure: N(omega,.)
 is a measure on R_+ x E (N-valued, possibly infinite); for every measurable A with (Leb (x) nu)(A) < oo,
 N(A) is Poisson((Leb (x) nu)(A)); for disjoint A_1..A_k the N(A_i) are independent.

What a faithful Lean structure must be.
- Data: the BM W (unit 04), the random measure N on (time) x E (each N(A) measurable), the initial
  sigma-algebra F_0 <= the ambient one; parameters nu (sigma-finite), E measurable.
- Properties: N is a PRM with intensity Leb (x) nu (Poisson marginals on finite-intensity sets and
  independence over disjoint sets, or an equivalent characterisation, e.g. Laplace functional);
  mutual independence of sigma(W), sigma(N) and F_0 (joint, not merely pairwise).
- Whether sigma-finiteness of nu and countable generation of E are fields, typeclass hypotheses or
  hypotheses of the propositions does not matter as long as they are not required more strongly than
  the paper (e.g. nu finite would be narrower) and not dropped in a way that changes the meaning (their
  absence would make the structure broader, harmless for definitions, but some downstream facts need
  them).
- The filtration (separate unit 14) must then be the usual augmentation of
  F_0 v sigma(W_s, s <= t) v sigma(N(A), A subset [0,t] x E).
- W must be a BM with respect to this filtration: implied by the independence of W from N and F_0
  together with W's independent increments; it is not an extra assumption.
