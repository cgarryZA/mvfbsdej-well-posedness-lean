Paper notion: none directly; the compensated-Poisson integral int_0^t int_E U_s(e) Ntilde(ds,de)
for U in H^2_N (sec. 3/4: "compensated-Poisson isometry") is the textbook L^2 extension from
simple (step) predictable integrands of the form
  psi_s(omega, e) = sum_{i,k} xi_{ik}(omega) 1_{(t_i,t_{i+1}]}(s) 1_{B_k}(e),
with B_k in E of finite nu-measure (so that Ntilde((t_i,t_{i+1}] x B_k) is square-integrable)
and xi_{ik} bounded (or square-integrable) F_{t_i}-measurable. "MarkStep" presumably is such an
elementary integrand: a time step (s in (a,b]) times a mark set B times an F_a-measurable
coefficient. Faithful Lean: a structure with times a <= b (or a partition), a mark set B
measurable with nu(B) < oo, and a coefficient that is F_a-measurable (adapted, w.r.t. the
filtration of the integral, in which N's increments after a are independent of F_a) and bounded
or L^2. Harmless: one step (a,b] x B per object with sums taken elsewhere; closed vs open time
endpoints. Not faithful: no finiteness of nu(B) (then Ntilde of the step is undefined / not L^2
under infinite activity), no adaptedness.

Paper context (sec. 2, sec. 3, sec. 4, App. A). Standing basis: (Omega,F,(F_t),P) satisfying the usual
conditions, horizon T>0, d-dimensional Brownian motion W and an independent Poisson random
measure N on R_+ x E with intensity dt nu(de), nu sigma-finite on a countably generated (E,E);
(F_t) is the usual augmentation of the filtration generated by W, N and an independent initial
sigma-field F_0. Stochastic integrals int Z dW (Z in H^2_W: R^{m x d}-valued predictable,
