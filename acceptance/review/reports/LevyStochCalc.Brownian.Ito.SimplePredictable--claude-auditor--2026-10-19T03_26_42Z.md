Paper notion. The paper does not define simple integrands; it uses the Ito integral int_0^t Z_s dW_s
for Z in H^2_W (sec. 3) "by the Ito isometry" (Lemma bpbsde proof), i.e. the standard L^2
construction: the integral is first defined on simple (elementary) predictable processes and
extended by the isometry and density. So the notion is the textbook class of simple predictable
processes relative to the paper's filtration (F_t):
  phi_t(omega) = sum_{i<N} xi_i(omega) 1_{(t_i, t_{i+1}]}(t),
  0 <= t_0 < t_1 < ... < t_N (finite partition; within [0,T] or [0,oo)),
  each xi_i F_{t_i}-measurable and bounded (or square-integrable), real-valued (1-dim case).
What a faithful Lean definition must be:
 - a structure carrying a finite partition (N : Nat, times t_0..t_N, monotone/nondecreasing,
   nonnegative) and coefficients xi_i : Omega -> R;
 - an adaptedness condition: xi_i measurable w.r.t. F_{t_i} of the filtration fixed for the
   integral (one for which W is a Brownian motion, i.e. the paper's (F_t) or a filtration it is
   instantiated to) -- NOT w.r.t. a later time (no anticipation);
 - an integrability condition (bounded or L^2) so that the simple integral is in L^2 and the
   isometry E|int phi dW|^2 = E int |phi|^2 dt holds.
Representation choices that are harmless: closed-left vs open-left intervals (the simple integral
sum xi_i (W_{t_{i+1}} - W_{t_i}) is the same, and the processes agree dt x dP-a.e.); allowing
repeated partition points (a zero-length interval contributes nothing); bounded vs L^2
coefficients (both classes are dense in H^2 and give the same extension). Not harmless: missing
adaptedness, coefficients measurable only w.r.t. the ambient sigma-algebra, or no integrability
(then the isometry, hence the extension, can fail).

Paper context (sec. 2, sec. 3, sec. 4, App. A). Standing basis: (Omega,F,(F_t),P) satisfying the usual
conditions, horizon T>0, d-dimensional Brownian motion W and an independent Poisson random
measure N on R_+ x E with intensity dt nu(de), nu sigma-finite on a countably generated (E,E);
(F_t) is the usual augmentation of the filtration generated by W, N and an independent initial
sigma-field F_0. Stochastic integrals int Z dW (Z in H^2_W: R^{m x d}-valued predictable,
E int_0^T |Z|_F^2 dt < oo) and int int U dNtilde (U in H^2_N) are the L^2 (Ito /
compensated-Poisson isometry) integrals; they are true L^2 martingales, cadlag, and are used in
identities holding a.s. for every t (sec. 3, "solution"). The paper never constructs these
integrals itself: it takes the standard construction (Applebaum sec. 4, He-Wang-Yan). So every
unit below is infrastructure: the paper's notion is the textbook one, and a faithful Lean
definition is one that (together with the other definitions) yields the textbook object on the
paper's basis for every integrand the paper's claims use; junk values outside that class are
harmless only if every proposition restricts to the class.
