HH = S^2(R^n) x S^2(R^m) x H^2_W x H^2_N with
||Theta||^2 = E sup|X|^2 + E sup|Y|^2 + E int (|Z|_F^2 + ||U||^2) dt;
elements are equivalence classes (cadlag components up to indistinguishability, Z up to
dt x dP-a.e., U up to dt x dP x nu-a.e.); under this identification ||.|| is a norm and HH is a
Banach space. The clause is completeness.

- the statement CompleteSpace (or every Cauchy sequence converges in that norm with limit in the

  norm, fine. Completeness of the S^2 factor is the nontrivial part (limit is cadlag adapted, via
  a.s. uniform convergence along a subsequence).

the standing filtration enlJoint: X, Y adapted on [0,T], every path right-continuous on [0,T),

supSize < inf. supSize = E sup_{[0,T]} |X|^2 + E sup |Y|^2 + sum_{k,j} E int_0^T Z_kj^2 +
sum_k E int_0^T int_E U_k^2 dnu ds, which is exactly ||Theta||^2_HH (Frobenius and L2(nu) norms
in coordinates). The S^2 requirement E sup < inf is the supSize bound; Hweak membership is then
implied.

Statement. For every sequence of such tuples that is Cauchy for supSize of differences (i.e. for
||.||_HH, squared norm Cauchy iff norm Cauchy), there is a tuple of the same class with every
path cadlag (left limits for every path on (0,T], right-continuity from PaperWeakTuple), finite
supSize, and supSize(Theta_j - Theta) -> 0. This is completeness of (HH, ||.||_HH): every Cauchy
sequence converges in the norm to an element of HH. Arbitrary standing basis, dims, sigma-finite
nu, T.

The paper's equivalence classes are not formed as a quotient type; completeness is stated on
representatives. Since the identification (indistinguishability, a.e.) is exactly the kernel of
the seminorm supSize, sequential completeness of the seminormed space of representatives is
equivalent to completeness of the quotient normed space. The limit is required to have the same
measurability (adapted, predictable, marked predictable), so the limit lies in HH and not merely
in some larger L2 space. Hypotheses are met (witness sec3_HH_complete_hypotheses: a Cauchy
sequence over a Levy driver with nu = delta_0), not vacuous.
