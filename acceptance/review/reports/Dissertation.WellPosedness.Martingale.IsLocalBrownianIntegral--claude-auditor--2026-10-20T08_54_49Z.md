# Reading (locked before the Lean): IsLocalBrownianIntegral

Paper's notion. The manuscript does not define a "local Ito integral" in words; it writes
stochastic integrals int_0^t H_s . dW_s (int_0^t <y_{s-}, Z_s dW_s> etc.) whose integrands are
predictable (or progressive) and only locally square-integrable (int_0^T |H_s|^2 ds < inf a.s.,
e.g. dZ^T dY_- with dY in S^2, dZ in H^2_W), and calls them local martingales (Lemma 4.1 proof,
lines 139-147; Lemma 4.3 proof, lines 579-612: "those four local martingales"). Reference for the
calculus: Applebaum section 4 (cited for Ito's formula) -- the standard Ito integral for
predictable integrands with int_0^T |H|^2 ds < inf a.s., defined by localisation of the L^2 Ito
integral; it is a continuous local martingale of the filtration (F_t) in which W is a Brownian
motion. The filtration is the usual augmentation of the one generated by W, N and F_0.

What a faithful Lean definition must be. A predicate on a process M : [0, T] x Omega -> R (or
indexed by R/R_{>=0}), a (vector) integrand H and the Brownian motion W such that:
 1. Existence/identification: there are (F_t)-stopping times tau_k, nondecreasing, with
    tau_k >= T eventually a.s. (or P(tau_k >= T) -> 1), with H 1_{[0, tau_k]} in the L^2 Ito class
    (predictable, E int_0^T |H|^2 1_{s <= tau_k} ds < inf), such that for each k the stopped
    process M_{t ^ tau_k} equals the L^2 Ito integral of H 1_{[0, tau_k]} at time t, a.s. for every
    t in [0, T] (or indistinguishably); and M_0 = 0. Any equivalent characterisation is fine
    (e.g. M continuous local martingale with <M, W^j> = int H^j ds for all j, M_0 = 0).
 2. Uniqueness up to indistinguishability on [0, T]: two processes satisfying the predicate for the
    same H must be indistinguishable on [0, T]. This needs path regularity (continuous or cadlag
    on [0, T]) in the predicate, or the conclusion of indistinguishability from a.s. equality at
    each time plus regularity. If the predicate only fixes M at each time a.s. without path
    regularity, M is pinned down only up to modification, which is weaker than the paper's
    integral (the paper's integral is a continuous process; sup_{t<=T} |M_t| is sensitive to
    modifications).
 3. Local martingale in the paper's filtration (F_t) (the augmented filtration generated by W, N,
    F_0), with localising sequence exhausting [0, T]; adapted.
 4. Junk: if H is not locally square-integrable the predicate should be unsatisfiable or at
    least not assign a spurious meaning used by the propositions; edge s = 0 irrelevant.
Not acceptable: a predicate that is satisfied by M = 0 for any H, or that ties M to H only in
expectation/quadratic-variation norm without fixing the process; the L^2 integral it relies on
must be the genuine Ito integral (isometry and agreement on simple integrands) in the paper's
filtration.

## Standing setting
Standing setting (manuscript section 2, manuscript/paper/sections/02-model-assumptions.tex lines 1-11): a filtered
probability space (Omega, F, (F_t), P) satisfying the usual conditions, horizon T > 0, dimensions
n, m, d >= 1, a d-dimensional (F_t)-Brownian motion W and an independent Poisson random measure N
on R_+ x E with intensity dt nu(de), nu sigma-finite on a countably generated (E, \mathcal E);
(F_t) is the usual augmentation of the filtration generated by W, N and an independent initial
sigma-field F_0. Ñ = N - dt nu. (2.1): E int int psi dN = E int int psi nu(de) ds in [0, inf] for
predictable psi >= 0. Spaces (section 3): S^2 = cadlag adapted with E sup_{t<=T} |V_t|^2 < inf;
H^2_W = predictable R^{m x d} with E int_0^T |Z|_F^2 < inf; H^2_N = predictable L^2(nu;R^m)-valued
with E int_0^T ||U||^2 < inf, read through a P (x) E-measurable representative ((2.8), Fubini
isometry). c_BDG (section 4, lines 22-37): one universal constant, valid for cadlag local
martingales, at p = 1 (Davis form, quadratic-variation form) and p = 2; it is fixed before all
data (C's depend on it). "Supremum criterion": a cadlag local martingale with integrable running
supremum has zero-mean increments.

Item by item against the locked reading.

1. Identification with the Ito integral. The predicate asks for stopping times tau_k of the given
   filtration, nondecreasing in k for every omega, with T <= tau_k eventually almost surely, such
   that each cut-off H_s 1_{s <= tau_k} is jointly measurable, progressively measurable and of
   finite energy on every horizon, and for every t in [0, T], almost surely on {t <= tau_k},
   M_t equals the L^2 Ito integral (LevyStochCalc MultidimBrownianMotion.stochasticIntegral, the
   sum over coordinates of the L^2 limits of the master simple integrals, an F_t-measurable
   representative at each time) of the cut-off at time t. This is the localisation of the L^2
   integral that the reading asks for. Requiring finite energy "on every horizon" is harmless:
   a localising sequence can be capped at T (tau_k ^ T >= T iff tau_k >= T), after which the cut-off
   vanishes beyond T. If H is not locally square integrable on [0, T] the predicate cannot hold
   (on {int_0^T |H|^2 = inf} the cut-off energy would be infinite once tau_k >= T), so no junk
   meaning is attached.
2. Uniqueness up to indistinguishability on [0, T]. M is required adapted and cadlag on [0, T]
   for every omega (right-continuous on [0, T), left limits on (0, T]). Given two processes
   satisfying the predicate (sequences tau, tau'), the locality of the L^2 Ito integral (the
   integrals of two integrands agreeing on [[0, rho]] agree at t on {t <= rho}) gives
   M_t = M'_t a.s. for each t in [0, T]; right-continuity on [0, T) and the value at T then give
   indistinguishability on [0, T]. So the predicate pins down the paper's integral up to
   indistinguishability on [0, T]. (M_0 = 0 a.s. follows from the identification at t = 0.)
3. Local martingale in the paper's filtration. Not stated as a conjunct, but it follows: with
   cadlag versions, M_{t ^ tau_k} equals the L^2 integral of the cut-off on [0, T] (locality and
   the identification for k' > k on {tau_k < T}), which is an F-martingale; tau_k exhausts [0, T].
   The filtration is a parameter; the propositions of this chunk instantiate it with
   enlJoint G0 D hG0, the usual augmentation (right-continuous version, measurable null sets added)
   of the joint natural filtration of (W, N) joined with the independent initial sigma-field G0,
   i.e. the paper's filtration of section 2. Existence of such M is supplied by
   exists_isLocalBrownianIntegral under the usual conditions that enlJoint satisfies.
4. Edge cases. Integrand values at s <= 0 and s > T do not affect the integral on [0, T]
   (energy over [0, T'], cut-offs). Progressive (rather than predictable) cut-offs: the Brownian
   L^2 integral of a progressive dt-square-integrable integrand is that of its predictable version
   (they agree dt dP-a.e.), so the domain is the paper's up to null sets.
