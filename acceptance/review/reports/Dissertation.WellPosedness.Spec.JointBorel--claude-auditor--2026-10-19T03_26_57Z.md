UNIT def:Dissertation.WellPosedness.Spec.JointBorel
Paper notion (§2, after the signatures): "All coefficients are jointly Borel measurable in their
arguments; for the jump coefficient, whose values lie in the fibre, this is taken as Borel
measurability of the map (t,theta,mu) -> h(t,theta,.,mu), [0,T] x H x P_2(H) -> L^2(nu;R^n)"
(eq. 2.3). So: b, sigma, f jointly Borel on [0,T] x H x P_2(H); h Borel as a map into the Hilbert
space L^2(nu;R^n) (strong = Borel measurability, equivalent there since L^2(nu) is separable);
g jointly Borel on R^n x P_2(R^n).
What a faithful Lean definition must be: a predicate on the coefficient system requiring
Measurable (uncurried) b, sigma, h, f on the product of the time type, H and the measure type, and
Measurable (uncurried) g on R^n x P_2(R^n), with Borel sigma-algebras: on time the Borel (Lebesgue
measurable would be weaker - still acceptable only if the uses only need a.e.-measurability); on H
the Borel sigma-algebra of the norm topology (= product of the Borels, H separable); on the measure
argument the Borel sigma-algebra of P_2 with W_2 (which on P_2 of a Polish space coincides with
the trace of the Giry/weak sigma-algebra, so a Giry-type measurable structure is an acceptable
representation); for h, Borel measurability into Lp 2 nu (the L^2 element, not pointwise in e).
Product measurability of the uncurried map is required, not separate measurability in each
argument. Nothing more (no continuity) and nothing less.
