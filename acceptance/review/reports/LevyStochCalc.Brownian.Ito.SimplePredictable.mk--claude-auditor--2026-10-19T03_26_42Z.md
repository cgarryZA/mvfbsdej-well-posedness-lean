The anonymous constructor of the structure SimplePredictable. Its meaning is exactly the meaning
of the structure's fields and their constraints (see the reading of SimplePredictable): a finite
partition N, times t_0..t_N (monotone, nonnegative), coefficients xi_i, F_{t_i}-adaptedness and
the integrability condition. Faithful iff the structure is faithful; the constructor adds nothing
of its own (any field it requires beyond the textbook class would narrow the class, any missing
constraint would widen it beyond the processes for which the Ito isometry holds).

Paper context (sec. 2, sec. 3, sec. 4, App. A). Standing basis: (Omega,F,(F_t),P) satisfying the usual
conditions, horizon T>0, d-dimensional Brownian motion W and an independent Poisson random
measure N on R_+ x E with intensity dt nu(de), nu sigma-finite on a countably generated (E,E);
(F_t) is the usual augmentation of the filtration generated by W, N and an independent initial
sigma-field F_0. Stochastic integrals int Z dW (Z in H^2_W: R^{m x d}-valued predictable,
E int_0^T |Z|_F^2 dt < oo) and int int U dNtilde (U in H^2_N) are the L^2 (Ito /
compensated-Poisson isometry) integrals; they are true L^2 martingales, cadlag, and are used in
identities holding a.s. for every t (sec. 3, "solution"). The paper never constructs these
integrals itself: it takes the standard construction (Applebaum sec. 4, He-Wang-Yan). So every
unit below is infrastructure: the paper's notion is the textbook one, and a faithful Lean
definition is one that (together with the other definitions) yields the textbook object on the
paper's basis for every integrand the paper's claims use; junk values outside that class are
harmless only if every proposition restricts to the class.
