# Reading: spec lem_lawflow (Lemma A.1, existence clause)

Lemma A.1 (first clause): Let X, Y be cadlag adapted (R^n, R^m), Z (R^{m x d}) and U
(L^2(nu;R^m), via a P (x) E-measurable representative) fixed predictable versions, with
E int_0^T (|X_t|^2+|Y_t|^2+|Z_t|_F^2+int_E |U_t(e)|^2 nu(de)) dt < oo.
Then the a.e.-defined maps t -> Law(Theta^-_t) (Theta^-_t = (X_{t-},Y_{t-},Z_t,U_t)) and
t -> Law(Theta_t) admit Borel representatives mu^-, mu : [0,T] -> (P2(H), W2) with
mu^-_t = mu_t for a.e. t.

A faithful formal statement must contain:
* Setting: standing basis (usual conditions, filtration generated by W, N, F_0), T > 0, nu
  sigma-finite on countably generated E; H = R^n x R^m x R^{m x d} x L^2(nu;R^m) with the Hilbert
  norm (an equivalent norm gives the same Borel sets and the same P2, which is harmless for this
  clause, but W2 must be the W2 of a norm equivalent to |.|_H for "Borel" to mean the same thing).
* Hypotheses: X, Y cadlag (pathwise; a.s. is a harmless variant) and adapted; Z, U predictable;
  the integrability condition. (No coefficient hypotheses.)
* Conclusion: EXISTS mu, mu^- : [0,T] -> P2(H), Borel measurable for the W2 topology (Borel
  sigma-algebra of (P2(H),W2)), such that
  - for a.e. t in [0,T]: mu_t = Law(Theta_t) (the pushforward of P under omega -> Theta_t(omega),
    which lies in P2(H) for a.e. t by Fubini),
  - for a.e. t: mu^-_t = Law(Theta^-_t),
  - for a.e. t: mu^-_t = mu_t.
  "a.e." is w.r.t. Lebesgue measure on [0,T].
* The left limit at t = 0 is a convention (null set), harmless.
Vacuity/trivialisation risks: a P2 space whose measurable structure is trivial (top) or a "Law"
that is junk on a positive-measure set; the a.e. equalities must be with the real laws.
