sum_j int Z^j dW^j, with matrix integrands handled row by row). Faithful Lean: the sum over
coordinates j of the scalar Ito integrals of Z^j against W^j, where EACH scalar integral is
taken relative to a filtration in which the integrands of the paper are adapted -- the joint
filtration (at least the joint natural filtration of all d coordinates, and in the paper's use
the augmented filtration of W, N, F_0) -- and with respect to which each W^j is a Brownian
motion. If each scalar integral is relative to W^j's own natural filtration, then an integrand
adapted to the joint filtration (e.g. Z^1 depending on W^2) falls outside the class and gets
the junk value: not faithful. Isometry E|int Z dW|^2 = E int |Z|_F^2 must hold for the paper's
class. Junk outside the class harmless only under restriction.
