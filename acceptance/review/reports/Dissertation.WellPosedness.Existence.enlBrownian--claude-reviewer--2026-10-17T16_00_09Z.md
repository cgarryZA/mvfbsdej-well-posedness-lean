Paper notion (Ex 5.2, with Sec. 2): in the Brownian reduction the filtration is generated by the
Brownian motion W and an independent initial sigma-field F_0, with the usual augmentation:
F_t = the P-completion (augmentation by P-null sets) of F_0 v sigma(W_s : s <= t), made
right-continuous (usual conditions). For a Brownian filtration augmented by null sets,
right-continuity is automatic (Blumenthal 0-1 / PRP), so an augmentation by null sets of
F_0 v sigma(W_s, s<=t) is the right object. "enl" = enlarged (initial enlargement by F_0).

Faithful Lean: a filtration on Omega (indexed by time in [0,T] or R>=0) with
F_t = sigma(F_0-generator, W_s for s <= t, null sets) for each t; F_0 must be the given
independent initial sigma-field (containing the sigma-algebra of xi_0); the Brownian motion W
must be the d-dimensional BM of the basis. Must be a sub-sigma-algebra of the ambient one and
monotone in t. If null sets are not included the notion is the raw natural enlargement (a
representation choice that may matter for adaptedness of versions); if right-continuity is not
built in it is harmless for the Brownian case given augmentation.

Against my reading:
- generated by W (all d coordinates) and the initial sigma-field G0 at every time: yes.
- augmented by null sets: yes, by the P-null sets of mOmega (no completion of mOmega). The
  paper's "usual conditions" on a possibly non-complete space is read the same way; no result of
  the paper depends on adding non-measurable subsets of null sets. Representation choice.
- right-continuity: built in, by taking the right-continuous version before augmenting. The
  augmentation of F_{t+} equals the right-continuous version of the augmented filtration (if
  A is in aug(F_s) for all s > t, choose A_k in F_{t+1/k} a.e. equal to A, limsup A_k is in F_{t+}
  and a.e. equal to A), so this is the usual augmentation. For a Brownian filtration it would be
  right-continuous anyway.
- time indexed by R: for t < 0 the filtration is the time-0 one; the Brownian motion of the
  library is a.s. 0 at negative times, so nothing is added. Harmless.
- independence of G0 from W is not part of the filtration; it is a hypothesis of the
  propositions (as in the paper: "independent initial sigma-field").
