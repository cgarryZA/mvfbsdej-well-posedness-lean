       + E sup_{t<=T} |int_0^t delta sigma dW|^2 + E sup_{t<=T} |int_0^t int_E delta h Ntilde|^2 ];

||Theta||_S^2 = E sup|X|^2 + E sup|Y|^2 + E int (|Z|_F^2 + ||U||^2). The expectations must be
the true ones (all are finite here, or the chain must be read in [0, infinity]); a Lean
expectation that takes a junk value 0 on a non-integrable function, or a real supremum that is
0 on an unbounded set, would not be faithful unless integrability/boundedness is established.

Constants. c_BDG is the paper's one universal Burkholder-Davis-Gundy constant (quadratic
variation form, valid at p = 1 and p = 2 for cadlag local martingales). Any constant larger
than an admissible BDG constant is admissible, so "there is c_0 > 0 such that for every
c >= c_0 the chain holds with c_BDG := c" is a faithful reading PROVIDED c_0 is quantified
first, before T, the dimensions, the probability space, the coefficients, lambda, q and the
trials (the paper's c_BDG is universal and the paper stresses that no dimension enters). If
c_0 may depend on the data (in particular on Theta^1, Theta^2), steps (ii) and (v) become
nearly trivial by taking c_0 large, and the statement is weaker than the paper's: a mismatch.
C_X depends only on T, L, c_BDG, given by the explicit formula.

Breadth. The chain does not need lambda in [0,1] (any real lambda works) nor Assumption 2.2
except for Xbar to be well defined; a Lean statement for all real lambda or with fewer
hypotheses is broader (instantiate lambda in [0,1] and the Lemma 4.2 hypotheses). Trials in a
class wider than S would also be broader. A hypothesis beyond Lemma 4.2's (e.g. Assumption 2.5,
extra integrability of the trials not implied by membership in S, bounded coefficients) is a
narrowing. The hypotheses must be satisfiable by an instance the paper intends (e.g. linear
Lipschitz coefficients, any trials in S, e.g. Theta^1 = Theta^2 = 0, and a nonzero instance).
