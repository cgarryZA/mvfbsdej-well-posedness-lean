Paper notion: the interior Lipschitz bound of Assumption 2.3 at one fixed time t: for all
Theta, Theta' in L^2(Omega,F_t;H) (square-integrable, F_t-measurable tuples on the standing basis),
with mu = Law(Theta), mu' = Law(Theta'), almost surely
  |b(t,Theta,mu)-b(t,Theta',mu')| + |sigma(...)-sigma(...)|_F + ||h(...)-h(...)||_{L^2(nu;R^n)}

joint law of the pair - acceptable as a representation choice only with that justification.
