Paper notion. §2: N is a Poisson random measure on ℝ_+ × E with intensity dt⊗ν, and
(F_t) is the usual augmentation of the filtration generated by W, N and an independent F_0;
implicit is that N is an (F_t)-Poisson random measure: (i) adaptedness — for every
measurable A ⊆ E (with ν(A) < ∞) and s ≤ t, N((s,t] × A) (equivalently N on Borel sets of
[0,t] × E) is F_t-measurable; (ii) independence of the future — for every s, the family
{N(B) : B ⊆ (s,∞) × E measurable} is (jointly) independent of F_s. This is what makes Ñ
integrals of predictable integrands martingales, the isometry hold and the compensation
formula hold.

Faithful Lean predicate (on a filtration ℱ and a PRM N): (i) and (ii) for all s ≤ t, all A
(at least those of finite ν-mass, which suffices by σ-finiteness and monotone limits), with
(ii) as independence of the σ-algebra generated by the increments after s (or for each finite
family of disjoint increments jointly) from ℱ_s. Independence of each single increment
separately from ℱ_s is strictly weaker than (ii) in general — that would be a defect unless
compensated by the PRM's own independence (I would check whether the martingale property of
products/sums then still follows; generally joint independence of the future σ-algebra is
what's needed). Requiring the property for sets with ν(A) = ∞ is harmless (N(B) = ∞ a.s.).
It must be satisfiable by the paper's filtration (augmented, generated by W, N, F_0 with the
three independent).
