# Reading of the §2 PRP paragraph (sec2_prp)

Passage: "The filtration generated by W and N has the Brownian–Poisson predictable representation
property ... and it passes to the independent initial enlargement by F₀ in the standard way:
every square-integrable (F_t)-martingale M can be written as
M_t = M₀ + ∫_0^t Z_s dW_s + ∫_0^t ∫_E U_s(e) Ñ(ds,de), for every t ∈ [0,T],
for an F₀-measurable M₀ and a predictable square-integrable pair (Z,U), and this pair is unique
up to null sets."

A faithful formal statement must contain:
- Setting: the standing basis: d-dimensional Brownian motion W, Poisson random measure N with
  intensity dt⊗ν (ν σ-finite, E countably generated), independent; F₀ an initial σ-field
  independent of (W, N); (F_t) the usual augmentation of F^W ∨ F^N ∨ F₀; T > 0 (or any T).
- Hypothesis: M a real (or ℝᵐ) square-integrable (F_t)-martingale on [0,T] (E|M_t|² < ∞ for
  t ≤ T; a càdlàg version may be assumed).
- Existence: Z predictable (or a version a.e. equal to one) ℝ^{1×d}-valued with
  E∫_0^T|Z|² dt < ∞, U 𝒫⊗𝓔-measurable with E∫_0^T∫_E|U|² ν dt < ∞, such that for every
  t ∈ [0,T], a.s., M_t = M₀ + ∫_0^t Z dW + ∫_0^t∫ U dÑ (L² stochastic integrals). M₀ is
  F₀-measurable (automatic for an adapted M).
- Uniqueness: any two such pairs (Z,U), (Z',U') satisfy Z = Z' dt⊗dP-a.e. and U = U'
  dt⊗dP⊗ν-a.e. on [0,T].
- Progressive instead of predictable integrands is a harmless representation if the
  integrals are the L² ones (every progressive L²(dt⊗dP) process agrees a.e. with a predictable
  one), but must be noted. The representation must be over the enlarged filtration (F₀
  included), not only the driver's natural filtration.
